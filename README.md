# << PARS-7 >>

**// PARHELION ADAPTIVE ROCKET SYSTEM — 7TH ITERATION**

A portable, adjustable-angle model rocket launch pad. 3D printed. Lightweight. Packable. Folds down to clip onto the back of a backpack.

`>> STATUS: OPERATIONAL`

## \\ MISSION PROFILE

Conventional launch pads are bulky, single-angle, and a pain to haul into the field. PARS-7 was designed from the ground up to solve all three: a dual-hinge core body gives you arbitrary launch angles, a two-legged stance keeps the footprint tight, and the whole thing folds into a pack-clippable assembly. Hardware cost is measured in pocket change.

---

## \\ KEY FEATURES

- `>>` **Adjustable launch angle** — dual-hinge core lets you fire from any angle, not just straight up
- `>>` **Two-legged design** — stable footprint without the bulk of a tripod
- `>>` **Packable** — folds into a compact assembly that clips to a backpack
- `>>` **Minimal hardware** — 3× M3 bolts, 3× M3 nuts, and a launch rod
- `>>` **Cheap to build** — almost entirely printed parts
- `>>` **Standard rod** — uses the Estes Maxi launch rod

## \\ SAFETY ADVISORY

`>> CAUTION //` Model rocketry carries real risk. Always follow your national model rocketry safety code (e.g. [NAR Model Rocket Safety Code](https://www.nar.org/safety-information/model-rocket-safety-code/) in the US), launch in an open area clear of dry brush, keep spectators at a safe distance, and never modify motors.

**PARS-7 is an unofficial DIY launch pad. You are responsible for verifying it is safe for your intended use before flying.**
